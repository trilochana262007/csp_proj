What smart fertilizer management means

Four practical decisions for efficient nutrient use.

The 4R nutrient stewardship principle

Use the right nutrient source, in the right quantity, at the right time and in the right place.

01 · Right SourceChoose a nutrient source suited to the crop and soil.
02 · Right RateMatch the quantity to soil-test and crop needs.
03 · Right TimeApply nutrients when the crop can use them.
04 · Right PlacePlace nutrients where roots can access them.

Learning centre

Core concepts and practices highlighted in the sustainable-farming project.

Fertilizer options

Understand the role of different nutrient sources.

🌿
Organic fertilizers

Compost and organic manure add organic matter and support soil structure.

🦠
Bio-fertilizers

Beneficial microorganisms can support nutrient availability and plant nutrition.

⏳
Slow-release fertilizers

Release nutrients gradually and can help reduce nutrient losses when properly selected and applied.

💧
Water-soluble fertilizers

Can be supplied through suitable irrigation systems such as drip fertigation when recommended.

Technology for smarter decisions

Tools described in the project report.

Soil testing kits

🛰️
GPS and drones

Can support precision mapping and targeted application.

📡
IoT sensors

Can monitor soil moisture and selected soil conditions in near real time.

📱
Farming apps

Can provide access to weather information, soil results and agricultural advisories.

Good practices to adopt

Simple actions that support long-term soil and water health.

🔬

Test before applying

Use soil-test results instead of relying only on fixed quantities or guesswork.

🌱

Build soil organic matter

Where suitable, include compost, organic manure and crop rotation in farm planning.

🌧️

Prevent runoff

Follow recommended timing and placement; avoid application when heavy rain is expected.

About the project

Smart Fertilizer Use for Sustainable Farming

Project purpose

This community-focused project explores efficient fertilizer practices that protect soil health and the environment while supporting agricultural productivity. The report describes farmer interactions, a fertilizer-use survey, awareness activities and recommendations for sustainable nutrient management.

FocusBalanced fertilizer use
ApproachSurvey and awareness
PrioritySoil health
OutcomeSustainable farming

Recommendations from the report

Short-term and long-term action areas.

Short-term actions

1
Awareness campaigns

Use posters, banners and community sessions to explain balanced fertilizer use.

2
Soil-testing drives

Organize accessible soil-health testing and explain the results.

3
Field demonstrations

Demonstrate composting and suitable organic or bio-fertilizer practices.

4
Local-language materials

Share practical guides on dosage, timing and safe use.

Long-term actions

1
Agricultural information website

Provide crop-wise guidance, smart fertilizer information and scheme updates.

2
Farmer training groups

Work with agricultural universities and Krishi Vigyan Kendras.

3
Digital advisory tools

Encourage useful mobile apps and SMS-based advisory services.

4
Community composting

Promote composting units and farmer-to-farmer knowledge sharing.
